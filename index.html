Discipline02

Sistem Merit & Demerit (SMDS)

The group's first merit and demerit scheme across all six units. A shared core applies to everyone and unit modules cover what differs between a kitchen and a resort. Supervisors submit entries through one form, and a dashboard reached by QR code lets every staff member see their own record for the first time.

OutcomeAll 105 staff can see their own conduct record for the first time, and supervisors score against one shared standard.

Structure
Core + Unit Module, weighted point bands

Reach

Evidence

Approach

How I work

The same three steps sit behind every case above, from the attendance audit to the merit and demerit rollout.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Audit before prescribing. Check the records, benchmark against the market, and find where the control actually fails.

    Attendance audit · revenue benchmarking
  2. 02

    Design

    Write it to the law and to the reader. Policy that holds up under Cap. 76, in language a supervisor can apply on shift.

    SOP Versi 2.0 · SMDS · compliance suite
  3. 03

    Embed

    Brief it, record it, automate it. Signed acknowledgements, one submission form, and dashboards staff can check themselves.

    On-site briefings · Apps Script · QR dashboard
